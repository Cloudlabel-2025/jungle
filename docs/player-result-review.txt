PLAYER RESULT REVIEW

MC: Week 1 > Results. CC: Week 1 & Recovery > Final Results & Allocation.
Use "Review a player's result", choose the player and Flight Level 1–5.

Inspect the saved conversation/claim check or mission answers, scoring checks
and experiments. Enter the evidence review and reason, acknowledge the review,
then choose:
- Validate current result: retain existing stars and record human validation.
- Correct result: set 1–3 stars and reconcile trophies/XP by the difference.
- Return for revision: revoke that level's completion/reward for revision.

No player energy is spent by a staff result decision. Existing team/replay
controls remain under their own disclosure. Flight stars are separate from
readiness rubric marks and publication; the Assessment Control workflow still
handles those marks. Validation does not publish marks or bypass staff signoff.

Only active, authorised CC/MC accounts with game:write can save. MC targets are
limited to assigned teams; CC targets stay in their cohort/college. Reviewing a
single player requires submitted evidence. Changes to the result or mission
evidence since loading are rejected for another review. Finalised publication
requires CC's existing correction revision before editing; the released Day 1
winner lock also remains in force.

Audit events retain the actor, reason, time, before/after result and reviewed
evidence revision. The latest review appears alongside the result; changed
evidence is labelled as a previous review. Previous audit events are preserved.

There is no AA role. The separate platform Super Admin has not been granted
college result-editing access by this feature.
