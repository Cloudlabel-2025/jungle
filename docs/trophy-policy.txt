TROPHIES, LEAGUES AND DIVISIONS — 24 NOVEMBER 2026

Conduct-based trophy penalties have been removed. No -5/-10/-25 deductions or
new trophy warnings can be created, including by older clients. HP and energy
remain the gameplay consequence systems; trophies recognise validated work.
Failed attempts, deadlines, asking for help and recovery do not cause trophy loss.

Below 300: Unranked, with no league badge or division.
Each earned league progresses I -> II -> III -> the next league's Division I.

League          Division I       Division II       Division III
Scout           300–449          450–599           600–749
Trailblazer     750–999          1000–1249         1250–1499
Ranger          1500–1999        2000–2499         2500–2999
Pathfinder      3000–3749        3750–4499         4500–4999
Guardian        5000–5999        6000–6999         7000–7999
Legend          8000–9999        10000–11999       12000+

The current account trophy balance determines both league and division. The
HUD, medal details, progress bar, staff correction preview and promotion /
demotion messages share one calculation. Medal stars and Roman numeral show
the current division. XP levels remain separate. There is no seasonal reset,
automatic decay or mission-entry trophy fee.

Only original result/reward correction can reduce trophies. MC/CC must review
the work and record the decision; invalid work loses its corresponding reward
without an additional conduct penalty. A correction can demote a division,
move to a lower league, or return the player to Unranked. All evidence and
previous decisions remain in the existing result audit.

Historical conduct receipts are retained. They are labelled as historical;
there are no new warning or deduction controls. CC may reverse an outstanding
historical deduction once, restoring only its actual debit. MC/players cannot
perform that reversal. Scope, stale-balance checks, duplicate protection and
the original/reversal receipts remain intact. No production balances are
automatically changed by this code update.
