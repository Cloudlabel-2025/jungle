PLAYER JOURNEY AND PAGE AUDIT — 24 NOVEMBER 2026

Scope: repository audit, local regression and an actual browser playthrough of
all five flight levels using fictional accounts and disposable data. Later
staff-managed expedition stages were not played end to end in the browser.

THE JOURNEY, START TO FINISH

1. College login, initial password change, scheduled check-in and flight welcome.
2. Flight Level 1: investigate a research question, then independently check a
   claim. Both missions must complete before Level 2 opens.
3. Flight Level 2: diagnose conflicting instructions and the lost signal.
4. Flight Level 3: investigate evidence and trace a system through the signal lab.
5. Flight Level 4: test claims and investigate access boundaries in the case deck.
6. Flight Level 5: revisit original research with a fresh conversation and
   complete the knowledge quiz. Required evidence controls level completion;
   effort and timing determine stars. These are separate from final marks.
7. MC reviews evidence/signs off; CC approves and publishes personal results.
8. Where required: attendance, recovery exams, verification and an approved
   recovery result. The flight's stars do not bypass this stage.
9. Staff approve and publish final teams. Player completes crash arrival and
   selects a character. This is the entry into the expedition chapter.
10. Approved team sprint work, evidence, independent review and accepted keys.
    The Alumni Connect map has five milestones. Later Part B workflows include
    persona demonstrations, private defence, marking, optional cross-team work
    and report review/issuance. Release and acceptance remain staff decisions.

ENERGY CONTRACT IMPLEMENTED

- Each NEW flight mission start costs 1 shared account energy. Level 1 charges
  research and claim-check separately; Levels 2–5 charge each activity start.
- A fresh player has 5 lives and 3 energy. The third spent energy removes a life
  and refills energy to 3, except at zero lives, where energy remains zero.
  The ten opening missions therefore leave a fresh player at 2 lives/2 energy,
  before any other penalties, recovery or authorised replay starts.
- The attempt, balance and energy ledger commit together. Invalid starts and
  rolled-back transactions cost nothing. Repeated start requests, page visits,
  autosaves, refinements and resumed attempts do not charge again.
- A staff-authorised fresh replay costs energy again. Historical attempts are
  not billed retroactively. Historical Level 1 claim checks remain usable.
- Spending energy is normal gameplay: it does not add conduct drops, clear
  streaks or count as a clean-day infraction. Zero lives creates one rescue case,
  including for players who do not yet have a published team.
- At zero energy players can finish existing work, read results and visit Help
  Desk. Mission exhaustion permits a three-question recovery drill; other cases
  require scoped staff approval with a recorded reason. Older zero-energy
  accounts can request a case even when none exists. Recovery restores three
  energy and at least one life, preserving higher life balances and progress.
  Repeated requests cannot refill twice. Recovery is recorded in the ledger.

FIXES IN THIS CHANGE

- Added transactional, idempotent energy spending for new flight missions and
  a separate explicit start for the Level 1 independent claim check.
- Exposed the cost before starting and added a Help Desk path at zero balance.
- Removed unnecessary loading of the expedition/staff Views module on entry.
- Load flight missions, crash arrival and equipment panels only when required.
- Move between finale cards immediately while preserving the save queue and
  flushing saved work before submission and navigation out of the mission.
- Reuse already-fetched attempt/level rows in programme snapshots, avoiding two
  duplicate database reads. Parallel reads are used outside Mongo transactions;
  transaction operations remain sequential.
- Corrected the generic activity hub's false 'Activity closed' label when
  completion-based progression permits a prepared activity to start.
- Added an explicit post-Level-5 handoff and useful explanations for gated pages.
- Replaced long learner forms with guided checkpoints: reflections, generic
  attempts, rules, support questions, briefing selection and challenge selection.
- Fixed legacy Level 1 content so research and independent claim checking both
  have playable entry points. Fixed the map covering the final results link.
- Made signal-lab answer navigation immediate while retaining queued saves.
- Kept check-in and journals editable after self-paced mission completion,
  subject to final publication locks. Verified SOD, EOD and Compass submission.
- Added player recovery drills, staff recovery approval, legacy case requests,
  scope checks, concurrent-request protection and audited restoration.

REMAINING PRODUCT WORK / RELEASE LIMITS

- The first four levels' authored content and game boards are retained. This
  change does not invent or replace their curriculum. Free-text research,
  explanation, reflection and evidence remain part of the educational workflow.
- Guided evidence entry still requires learner reasoning. The underlying
  authored curriculum has not been replaced or independently certified.
- Rehearse a real learner through MC review, CC publication, recovery when
  needed, final team publication and every later sprint. Automated fixtures
  verify controls but cannot certify teaching content or human assessment.
- Player drills and staff recovery were rehearsed with fictional accounts,
  including a legacy account with four lives, zero energy and no rescue case.
  Existing programme recovery exams and conduct decisions remain separate.
- Desktop and mobile-landscape results navigation were checked. Measure live
  navigation/input latency against the target hosting/database; no before/after
  production speed figure is claimed here.
- No production data migration, remote deployment, account changes or publication
  of a real cohort is performed by this change.
