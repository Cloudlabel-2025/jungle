# Personal player identity

Players create their own avatar from a camera face scan and customize its appearance, profile pose and background. The five former preset identities and their public portrait/camp assets are retired.

No saved avatar means initials or an unknown-player placeholder, never a generated preset portrait. A valid saved avatar supplies the profile, camp, mission and celebration appearance. Work roles do not select faces, clothes, gender or special character identities.

CMS, CC and MC account creation leaves players without a work role and leaves appearance creation to the player. Roles begin on entry to the jungle coding stage and rotate automatically when each subsequent weekly sprint is released. Published roster order fixes each player's seat; the five work roles cycle once across five sprints. Drafting or approving a sprint does not rotate live roles. The sprint records its role assignments, and previously released task ownership and evidence remain unchanged. Small teams retain distinct rotating roles, with MC coverage for vacant task seats. New accounts do not receive `characterKey` or `characterVariant`. Historical slot IDs remain readable in the task compatibility adapter so old evidence and published feature definitions continue to resolve; they are not selectable character identities.
