PLAYER WORK SAVING

Open the running game at http://127.0.0.1:3000. As a Player, use the profile
icon in the header, then View work by date.

Saved records belong to the signed-in player and cohort. Dates and times use
the cohort's time zone. The view shows clock-in/out sessions, manual versus
automatic clock-out, saved research selections/links, reflection drafts,
submitted reflections and activity work, and later expedition work.
Drafts do not submit work, award completion or bypass stage restrictions.
No assessment notes, reference answers or other players' work are exposed.

Research links, SOD/EOD reflections, Week 1 activity attempts and Code Lab
edits autosave after a short pause. Wait for the saved confirmation. Failed
saves are visibly labelled Not saved. Manual clock-out flushes pending work;
automatic clock-out retains all confirmed database saves. Drafts restore
when the editor is reopened. Concurrent stale drafts cannot overwrite a
newer saved revision. Persona changes cannot transfer a pending save to
another player.

Code Lab keeps the latest workspace checkpoint per date and attendance
session, with a downloadable JSON file. Later dates/sessions retain earlier
checkpoints. Saves without a confirmed active session are explicitly marked.
It is not a keystroke recorder. Old records that never stored a draft or
clock-out timestamp cannot be reconstructed; missing history is not invented.
