HELICOPTER TRAVEL — 13 NOVEMBER 2026

Selecting an available level or the mission-dock button starts one visual flight. The helicopter follows a curved arc and the camera follows from its departure position to the destination. The mission dialog mounts only on arrival. Repeated inputs and map panning are blocked while travelling. Cancel flight leaves the helicopter at its current position and never opens a delayed dialog. Closing a mission or cancelling returns keyboard focus to its level marker. Refresh does not auto-open a mission or award progress.

Locked markers retain their lock icon and aria-disabled state. Activation gives a status message, with no departure and no mission dialog. Availability is checked before departure, every frame and immediately before arrival, using the latest authenticated snapshot. A revoked target cancels travel. Existing server day/stage guards remain authoritative; no backend access, question locks, evidence or timer behavior was changed. Previously released CLOSED days remain available for their existing read-only views.

Motion defaults to the system reduced-motion preference. Reduced mode uses a short static transition; the wind control lets players explicitly enable full flight motion or reduce it again. Cancelling/unmounting clears animation callbacks. Switching personas or leaving the page cannot open an old mission later.
