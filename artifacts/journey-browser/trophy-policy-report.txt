TROPHY POLICY VERIFICATION — 24 NOVEMBER 2026

Approved policy: no league below 300. Scout 300, Trailblazer 750, Ranger 1500,
Pathfinder 3000, Guardian 5000, Legend 8000. Current balance determines rank.
Shared source: lib/trophy-leagues.js. Published conduct rules: docs/trophy-policy.txt.

New conduct mechanism
- Scoped MC/CC may record a warning or evidence-based fixed deduction.
- Missed agreed deadline -5; repeated violation after an earlier matching warning
  -10; human-verified plagiarism/fabrication -25. No caller-chosen amount.
- Require a unique incident reference, supporting evidence, reason and a current
  balance preview. Repeat cases reference a separate earlier warning for the
  same player and rule. Retry keys and incident keys prevent duplicate debits.
- Zero is the floor. Actual debit is min(rule amount, current balance), no debt.
- CC alone may reverse a deduction, once, restoring the actual debit rather
  than the nominal penalty. Original records are retained. XP, HP, energy,
  streaks and mission work are not changed by these decisions.
- Private in-app notifications link to the player's Help Desk decision receipts.
- Result corrections remain the mechanism for reversing invalid earned rewards.
  See the policy's sequencing guidance when conduct and result corrections overlap.
