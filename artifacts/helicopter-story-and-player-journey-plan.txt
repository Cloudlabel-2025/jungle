CODEWILD — CONFIRMED HELICOPTER STORY AND PLAYER JOURNEY PLAN
Recorded 12 November 2026; updated after the interactive research selection unit. Later story units remain planned.

AUTHORITATIVE USER DECISIONS
1. The Player's opening experience is travelling in a helicopter, not the jungle map.
2. The Player must not be shown all the existing modules on arrival.
3. On the first day, the Player has no character. The character space remains unknown.
4. Show seven random genres as interactive selectable cards. The latest user instruction replaces spinning with a selection game.
5. Player selects and explicitly locks a genre, then chooses and confirms ONE of its THREE questions, researches and submits the result.
6. Week 1 AI learning happens in the flight chapter. The helicopter crashes and the jungle map appears only AFTER Week 1 clearance AND final-team approval.
7. Replace the Player-facing key objective with collecting parts/tools used to repair the crashed helicopter.

These decisions supersede the five-card genre picker and automatic random-question assignment for FUTURE story-mode releases. Historical assignments, questions, evidence, marks and accepted project outcomes must remain pinned and readable.

STORY ORDER
Sign in -> helicopter arrival/prologue -> unknown character -> Day 1 briefing -> seven interactive genre cards -> one committed genre -> choose one of three questions -> research/evidence submission -> remaining Week 1 flight learning -> approved results/recovery if applicable -> approved/published final-team membership -> crash sequence -> jungle map -> accepted project milestones earn helicopter repair parts/tools -> final repair/extraction conclusion.

Do not imply that selecting a genre, submitting one question, reaching Friday's date or merely viewing a cutscene clears Week 1. Existing authoritative assessment, recovery, team-publication, sprint approval/loading and kickoff controls remain the gates for actual project work.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE BY STAGE

A. ARRIVAL / FLIGHT
Primary view: interactive sky map with a helicopter, connected flight levels and a visible Level 1 entry. The latest user correction puts boarding and research inside the map mission, rather than a standalone prologue followed by software pages.
Header: real learner name, an unknown/silhouette character slot, Help and Sign out. Do not display a fallback character, team role or ability.
Visible work: current flight/Week 1 task, required rules, agenda, relevant support and own receipts. Keep the larger module catalogue, Alumni project map, future sprints and character picker out of this stage.
Intro is skippable, accessible by keyboard and has a reduced-motion/static equivalent. Skipping animation never skips learning or approval gates.

B. DAY 1 — SELECT AND CHOOSE
1. Present the Day 1 purpose, instructions and availability after the existing required release/check-in flow.
2. Persist seven unique randomly offered available genres from the approved bank. No question previews before the genre is committed. Keep the separate five-person research groups for genre exclusivity; these are not final teams.
3. Player selects a card and explicitly locks it. Server validates current active user, cohort, open release, pinned research group and saved offer, then atomically commits that selected available genre.
4. Show the saved reservation and its three questions. Selecting a card alone does not commit it. Disable duplicate clicks. Reload/network retries return the same committed genre.
5. Reveal that genre's three approved questions. Player selects one. Explain that confirmation locks the question, then save it permanently for the round.
6. Show the selected prompt, acknowledgement and explicit Start research action. Preserve the existing 90-minute timer, draft/evidence submission, verification, revisions and assessment flow.
7. Show a clear submission receipt and next flight task/waiting state. Submission does not reveal the jungle or start the Alumni Portal project.
If a teammate claims an offered genre before confirmation commits, refresh only affected cards and return an understandable retry state. Never show a successful reservation for a claim that failed to save.
If the Player disconnects after the genre is saved but before choosing a question, resume the same genre and show its three questions; do not offer another genre.
Friday Fitness and the aligned Day 1 comparison must use the Player's CHOSEN question ID/text/version.

C. REMAINING WEEK 1 — FLIGHT LEARNING CHAPTER
Day 2 Explore, Day 3 STORY, Day 4 FIRE and Day 5 Prove remain the existing learning/assessment activities. Present them as current flight chapter tasks through one guided entry, not as a simultaneous catalogue of all modules.
Rules, announcements, Showcase, questions/support, Discovery, reflections and results remain available when needed, with a clear next action and return path.
Recovery participants retain their approved Saturday journey and private status. Show an explicit waiting/recovery instruction rather than an empty map or a premature crash.

D. CLEARANCE / TEAM GATE
Server checks the current published result and any approved recovery outcome, plus current approved/published final-team membership and valid programme state. No client flag, timestamp or animation completion may substitute for those records.
Only an eligible Player in the approved final roster can proceed into the crash chapter. Changes/corrections that invalidate eligibility must be handled explicitly; viewing the crash previously must not bypass revoked access.

E. CRASH / JUNGLE REVEAL
Play an accessible, skippable crash story sequence with a persistent completion receipt. Reload resumes safely and does not replay the full sequence on every login.
Then reveal the jungle map and the objective: work together to recover tools and parts, repair the helicopter and leave the jungle.
Proposed character reveal: open character selection/reveal at this stage, after final-team approval. Continue using existing character uniqueness rules within the approved team. First-day and flight-stage character selection endpoints must reject early access, not merely hide the button.
Keep narrative transition and project-work eligibility distinct: the map can explain that staff still need to approve/load a sprint or finish kickoff. Do not label a waiting sprint as playable.
Proposed default: the crash is a story event, not a learner failure or automatic life/energy penalty.

F. REPAIR PROGRESSION
Keep the five Alumni Portal milestones and their assessment/acceptance requirements. Award one distinct repair item for each accepted milestone, rather than a Player-facing Key 1–5.
Proposed illustrative item set (names/art can be refined): replacement cog, spanner/tool kit, wiring kit, fuel-system component, rotor component.
Award items only on authoritative team acceptance. Completing cards alone is not enough. Repeated acceptance/retries must not duplicate inventory. Reopened/revoked acceptance must have an explicit inventory/progression policy.
Display repair progress, collected items and the next needed component on the post-crash map. Finishing the required accepted milestones leads to a repair/extraction conclusion.
Keep assessment totals separate from story inventory. Preserve historical key-based outcomes and add a stable accepted-milestone-to-repair-item mapping; do not blindly replace every occurrence of the word “key” in source, keyboard guidance or database IDs.

MC AND CC EXPERIENCE
CC: configure story-mode release, seven-genre offer policy, approved question bank, separate five-person research groups and normal content/policies. Review assessment/publication/recovery/team gates. Configure the milestone-to-repair-item mapping and explicit transition status.
MC: deliver Week 1 flight learning, publish agendas/recaps, manage questions/Showcase/assessment reviews, record signoffs and monitor blocked learners. After approved team formation, conduct normal project kickoff and reviews.
Staff need a stage/status view showing the exact missing gate, responsible role and direct action. Avoid a manual “force crash/pass” switch that bypasses assessments or team approvals.
