LEVEL 1 / AIRBORNE DISCOVERY SCENE

Direction: Zelda-inspired exploration and quest discovery, using CODEWILD's
existing aerial artwork and green expedition palette. No third-party game
art, characters or copied screens are included.

Level 1 opens a full-screen mission scene. Seven world beacons replace the
genre-card grid. Selecting a beacon moves the helicopter and updates the
flight-radio briefing. Explicit confirmation reserves the genre. Three
question missions appear next; explicit confirmation locks the question.
The locked objective, acceptance, persistent timer and research-chat delivery
continue in the same scene. Accepting does not start the timer.

All reservations, five-person groups, question locks, deadlines, saved drafts,
review states and future-level release restrictions still use the existing
server APIs. Only presentation changed. No main-game player choices were
made or reset during testing. Attendance dialogs use a priority portal so
they remain above the full-screen scene. Other dialogs retain their layout.

Validation: 50 Day 1/research/attendance/saved-work regression tests passed,
zero skipped or failed. Isolated browser QA verified seven world choices,
selection feedback, genre reservation, three questions, explicit question
locking, no timer before Begin research, timer persistence, chat draft
autosave and restore after refresh, delivery awaiting review, and Level 2
remaining disabled. The attendance dialog was verified outside the map's
stacking context at its priority layer. Desktop and mobile were inspected;
final spacing and full-screen mobile fixes followed the initial review.

Final production build passed. The configured main game's Level 1 was opened
with its existing player progress and no genre/question reservation made.
The final mobile dialog measured 390 x 844 at x=0, y=0 with no horizontal
overflow. Final screenshots: flight-worlds-desktop.jpg and
flight-worlds-mobile.jpg. The main-game mission tab remains open for review.

Open http://127.0.0.1:3000/gamer/dashboard and enter Level 1. Returning players
resume their already locked question or research rather than selecting again.
