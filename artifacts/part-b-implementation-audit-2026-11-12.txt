   Source: 04_FINAL_FRIDAY/THURSDAY_TWO_LINKED_EXPORTS_TEMPLATE.json and FINAL_FRIDAY_OPERATOR_WORKTABLE.md.
   Code: lib/pilot-engine.js:2246-2283.
   Actual export includes aliases, commit SHA, persona, task IDs/statuses, core total and rubric version. It does not populate the required 13 criterion scores/maxima/evidence, task commit/test references, role rotation history, strengths/gaps, source artifact link, deployed test URL, prior-check evidence or known-defect dispositions. Downloading the complete reference template is not equivalent to exporting these actual learner/team records. Staff can supplement externally, but the generated export alone does not meet the frozen specification.

2. HIGH: Administrator's Chair bonus can be recorded before mandatory demo/defence.
   Source: nested PB-W6-ADV-ADMIN-01_Final_Friday_Config.json mandatory_prerequisites and timing; FF-ADV-12.
   Code: lib/pilot-engine.js:2356-2373.
   The handler checks CC permission, sprint/team membership, active state, voluntary participation, 0-5 bounds and duplication. It does not require the completed individual persona journey/private defence or validate the five individual criterion points, ten-minute session, isolated target and rollback evidence as structured inputs. Those evidence checks are currently delegated to the operator's URL/reason.
   Fresh test evidence: tests/pilot-part-b.test.mjs:155-170 successfully records +5 on the Monday fixture before the test begins demo/witness acceptance at lines 184 onward. This is a verified ordering gap, despite the test passing its current assertions.

3. HIGH: Imported questions lack a linked CC approval/version gate.
   Source: FINAL_FRIDAY_OPERATOR_WORKTABLE.md; PB-QA-095.
   Code: lib/pilot-engine.js:2298-2355; components/PilotWorkspace.js:1099-1140.
   Import accepts 1-12 questions per Gamer, stores advisory questions as AWAITING_HUMAN_REVIEW, and correctly avoids automatic grading. A separate staff action records artifact URLs and a reason. It does not transition a particular imported question version through MC review and CC approval, and final-demo-witness does not require that approved version. Manual external approval may supply the process, but the application does not enforce or link it. Exactly three questions with the frozen Q1/Q2/Q3 purposes are also not validated by import.

4. Reports and awards have supporting controls, with completion still dependent on manual work.
   Code: lib/pilot-engine.js:2374-2494; README.md:19-23.
   Awards record recommendations/CC decisions and enforce at most one criterion honour per Gamer. Selection of numerical highest-score specials and their source calculations is not performed by this handler; it accepts an operator-selected recipient and evidence. This requires witnessed manual verification for PB-QA-104/105/106.
   Reports accept externally prepared PDFs, assigned-MC review and CC issue after core results, a passing witness and Key 5. The application does not generate or inspect the exact two-page Passport/wording. Manual preparation is a supported path, but actual compliant PDFs and a real storage round trip have not been established by simulated metadata tests. Do not describe the frozen report templates as delivered learner reports.
